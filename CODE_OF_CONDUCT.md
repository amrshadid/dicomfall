# Code of conduct

DICOMFall is a place to learn and to help others learn. Everyone who takes part, in
issues, pull requests or anywhere else the project lives, is expected to:

- be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to DICOM or to open source;
- assume good intent, and ask before assuming;
- criticise ideas and code, never people;
- keep discussion on the work.

Harassment, insults, discrimination and personal attacks are not accepted.

If something goes wrong, contact the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are handled
privately. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and block anyone who doesn't
follow this code.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
